## Main video (horizontal, English): `la-canasta-presentacion-wide-en.mp4`

Title:
What is La Canasta? Argentina's supermarket price index, measured every day

Description:
La Canasta is an independent supermarket price index for Argentina, measured every single day. Every morning a program goes into the websites of Carrefour, Jumbo, ChangoMás, Día and Coto, saves more than 10,000 prices and follows 567 products of 33 kinds. It compares each one with the same product, in the same shop, the day before, and groups them like INDEC, with the official weights.

In this video: what it is, how the program works, what you find on lacanastaindex.com (the index of the day, the supermarket ranking, the price of every product, weekly and monthly reports and open data) and the 7 AI agents that look after it: the Guardian, the Reviewer, the Auditor, the INDEC Watcher, the Assistant, the Verifier and the Filmmaker.

Open data, free for everyone: https://lacanastaindex.com
Founded by Ramon Pepa.
